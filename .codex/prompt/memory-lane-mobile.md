## Task Spec
### Goal
- Define a plan to validate the digital memorial concept and design a prototype for a QR-scanned memorial profile app on iOS and Android.

### Context (what we know)
- The app scans a QR code on a gravestone.
- Scanning opens a webpage with a profile of the deceased and their life.
- The user wants to start with validation, then design a prototype.
- Target platforms are iOS and Android.
- Target audience is families and cemetery visitors; distribution likely via funeral homes.
- Monetization is B2B2C: charge funeral homes who pass along the service.
- Brand/tone preference: light blues and olive to dark.

### Assumptions (what we are inferring)
- The QR code resolves to a unique profile URL.
- The initial prototype can be web-based to validate the experience before native builds.

### Constraints
- Languages/frameworks/versions:
- Do not change:
- Performance/latency targets:
- Security/compliance constraints:

### Deliverables
- A validation plan (user interviews, assumptions to test, success metrics).
- A prototype plan (user flow, key screens, and content structure).
- A lightweight wireframe outline for the memorial profile webpage.
- A simple visual direction note using light blues and olive tones.

### Execution Plan (agent steps)
1) Identify key assumptions and risks for the concept.
2) Draft a validation plan with interview targets, questions, and success metrics.
3) Define the core user flow from scan to memorial page.
4) Outline the prototype structure (screens/sections) and content model.
5) Propose a minimal visual direction consistent with the color preferences.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Validation plan includes at least 3 assumptions, 10 interview questions, and measurable success criteria.
- Prototype plan includes end-to-end flow and key page sections.
- Wireframe outline covers essential memorial content and interaction points.
- Visual direction references light blues and olive tones.
